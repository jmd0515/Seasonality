Salon Seasonality

Where each salon's demand actually rises and falls across the year. Each line is a 4-week average indexed to that salon's own baseline, so a salon doing 200 customers a week and one doing 450 can be compared on shape. Above 100 is a busy stretch for that salon; below 100 is a slow one.

Month by month

Each salon's average week in a given month, against its own baseline. Bars above the line are months that run hot; below the line, months that run light. Where a month has occurred twice in the data, both are averaged.

Method. One row per completed ticket in the daily Customer Experience (Beta) reports (No Show, Cancelled and Void excluded), grouped into Saturday–Friday weeks. Weeks containing a holiday closure are scaled to a 7-day equivalent so a closed day reads as a closure rather than a slowdown. The plotted value is a trailing 4-week average. Baseline = 100 is each salon's average week over June 2025–May 2026, a full year.

Two things to know. North River Ranch opened in December 2025, so its baseline is its own Dec 2025–Sep 2026 average rather than a full year — its line is dashed for that reason and should be read for shape, not compared to the others' baselines. Its December figure is an opening surge, not a seasonal pattern; it will take another winter to know which. And six dates had been downloaded twice into the report folder; those duplicates are excluded here, which is why September–October 2025 and January–February 2026 sit lower than on the earlier version of this page.
